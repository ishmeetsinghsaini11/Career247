Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is the Career 247 Refer & Earn program?

It is a referral program where you can earn cashback by referring friends to enroll in Career 247 programs.

How much referral cashback will I receive?

You will receive ₹5,000 cashback for every successful referral across all programs.

How much discount will my friend receive?

Your friend will receive a ₹5,000 discount on the program they enroll in.

Who is eligible to participate in the Refer & Earn program?

All registered Career 247 users and students are eligible to participate.

How do I refer someone?

You can share the website link and provide the required details including your registered mobile number and email ID (the same as used in Career247), along with your friend’s referral details such as full name, phone number, email ID, and the program your friend is interested in (select from the list).

When is a referral considered successful?

A referral is considered successful once your friend:
Enrolls in a Career 247 program, and

Completes the verification process successfully.

How long does it take to receive the cashback?

The cashback is credited within 45 working days after successful enrollment and verification.

How will I receive my cashback?

After verification, Career 247 will share a Google Form to collect your bank details. The cashback will be credited directly to your bank account.

Is there any limit on the number of referrals?

No, there is no limit on the number of referrals you can make.

Is there any bonus for multiple referrals?

Yes. If you complete 5 successful referrals and have not withdrawn your cashback, you will receive an additional ₹5,000 bonus, making your total earnings ₹30,000.

Is the referral cashback applicable to all programs?

Yes, the referral benefit applies to all Career 247 programs, unless stated otherwise.

Can I refer an existing Career 247 student?

No. Referral benefits are applicable only for new enrollments.

Will I receive cashback if my friend cancels or takes a refund?

No. Cashback is applicable only for successful, verified, and non-refunded enrollments.

Can the cashback be transferred to someone else?

No. Cashback is non-transferable and will be credited only to the referrer’s bank account.

Who can I contact for referral or cashback-related queries?

For any referral or cashback-related assistance, you can contact the Career 247 support team through official channels or reach out on +919211610076
